# PrismaDiamond.github.io

Legal notice (Impressum) and privacy policies for my apps, served by GitHub Pages
at <https://prismadiamond.github.io/>.

| Page | URL |
| --- | --- |
| Impressum (de) | `/impressum/` |
| Legal notice (en) | `/legal-notice/` |
| Quevia – Datenschutzerklärung (de) | `/quevia/datenschutz/` |
| Quevia – Privacy policy (en) | `/quevia/privacy/` |

These URLs are compiled into the Quevia app and entered in Google Play and App
Store Connect. Do not move or rename them without shipping an app update first.

Plain HTML, no build step. `.nojekyll` stops GitHub Pages from running Jekyll.
Nothing is loaded from third parties: no fonts, no scripts, no analytics. Keep it
that way, the privacy policy says so.
